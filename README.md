## 🚀 How to Open
1. Open the folder **`jie-birthday-website`** on your Desktop.
2. Double-click the file **`index.html`** to open in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari).

---

## ✨ Website Features

1. **🎁 Pure Minimalist Gift Box Intro:**
   - Ultra-clean intro with only the gift box and the tag `"To: Jie"`.
   - Tap the gift box to open it and burst monochrome confetti into the birthday celebration.

2. **💿 Vinyl Disc Player with Real-Time Synced Lyrics:**
   - Plays the Korean birthday song audio from the YouTube Short (`https://youtube.com/shorts/tSzsjx-i5vY`).
   - The vinyl disc spins smoothly during playback.
   - Synced lyrics in Hangul, Romanization, and English automatically highlight in real-time.
   - Click any lyric line to seek directly to that part of the song.

3. **🎂 Interactive Cake & Tactile Ticket Pull:**
   - Tap the candles to blow them out and reveal `"Wish granted— Claim it now!"`.
   - **Tactile Ticket Pull**: Drag / swipe the ticket handle to the right (or tap) to tear the ticket and advance to Page 2.

4. **🎈 Game Hitbox Balloon Pop & Wish Writing:**
   - Three floating balloons: **Balloon 01**, **Balloon 02**, and **Balloon 03**.
   - Tap each balloon to hit it:
     - Shows game hitbox combo popups: `x1`, `x2`, `x3`, and `x4 CRITICAL!`.
     - On the 4th hit (`x4`), the balloon pops with a burst sound and confetti explosion!
   - Reveals the **Paper Wish Slip** where Jie writes her own wishes:
     - Text area for Jie to type her personal wish.
     - Tap **"Seal this Wish ✨"** to seal it with celebratory chimes and confetti.
     - Automatically saved to browser `localStorage` so her wishes persist even after reloading.
     - Includes "Edit wish" and "Pop balloon again" buttons.

5. **💌 Wax-Sealed Birthday Letter for Jie:**
   - Tap the minimalist wax-sealed envelope to slide out your sweet personal birthday message for Jie.

---

## ✏️ How to Edit in `config.js`
Open **`config.js`** using **Notepad**:
- **`balloonPrompts`**: Customize the placeholder text or guides for each balloon wish.
- **`letter`**: Contains your exact personal message for Jie.
